# R27 manual macro proof

Eight canonical scenarios have passing outcomes on two real native clients.
There are nine retained attempts: eight pass and the first held-repeat fixture
fails. [Receipts and hashes](manual-macros.json) identify the exact R27 client,
source, raw reports, handoff and unchanged 1280 x 720 screenshots.

- Held Anchor with Switch Back completes two observed place/charge/detonate
  cycles and consumes exactly two anchors and two glowstone. The corrected
  stationary fixture uses actual explosion knockback resistance 1, restored to
  0 afterward. The original failure is preserved: blast knockback moved the
  actor out of reach after it had already placed and charged a second anchor.
- Switch Back off completes one cycle without starting another while the
  detonation item remains selected.
- Creative Crystal placement preserves the real inventory; cap 0 prevents
  breaking that crystal. A separate real contact beyond three blocks removes
  the original entity within the creative reach of five.
- Creative Anchor completes its cycle without a totem or consumed inventory.
- Survival Anchor consumes a real totem and survives. The authoritative used
  statistic is 1, health changes from 20 to 2, and the native screenshot shows
  the Postmortal toast. Raw EntityEvent 35 is not exposed or claimed.
- Default entity/block ranges reject the distant actions; server attributes 4
  and 6 permit actual entity removal and block placement.
- A nearer living entity blocks Crystal targeting; Anchor's separate block-only
  ray completes the existing-anchor control.

Final cleanup verifies both native and server players at 20 HP, empty items,
effects and inputs, all modules off, original macro settings restored, default
ranges and explosion knockback resistance restored. Control streams close and
the fixture lock is removed before handing the pair back.

These cases use Single mode, delay 2 and Safe Anchor off. They do not verify
Double mode, every guard geometry, custom components, public servers or Internet
latency. Native Protect Loot uses the documented death-zone adaptation. Images
are original framebuffer captures; they are not reference graphics evidence.
